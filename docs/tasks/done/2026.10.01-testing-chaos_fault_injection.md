## 1. Goal

Build reusable chaos and fault-injection primitives in `gunz_utils.faults` for testing adversarial conditions—such as cancellation at arbitrary `await` boundaries, partial/corrupted I/O reads/writes, filesystem rename/fsync errors, and clock skew—without external mocking libraries.

## 2. Scope

### In Scope

- **Await Boundary Interceptor (`AwaitBoundaryChaos`)**:
  - Context manager that injects `asyncio.CancelledError` or custom transient exceptions at designated await points (e.g. 1st, nth, or probabilistic await transitions).
- **Filesystem & Stream Faults (`FaultyStream`, `FaultyFS`)**:
  - Simulates truncated reads, short writes, `EIO`, disk full (`ENOSPC`), and atomic rename collisions to test write atomicity and CAS rollback behavior.
- **Clock Drift Harness (`VirtualClock`)**:
  - Controllable deterministic monotonic/UTC clock provider supporting instantaneous forward time jumps, non-monotonic stalls, and deadline expiration testing.
- **Verification Suites**:
  - Exercise `atomic_write`, `ContentAddressedStore`, `AsyncBulkhead`, and `run_with_lease_heartbeat` under chaos conditions.

### Out of Scope

- Kernel-level network partition injection (iptables/tc).
- Heavy hypervisor/container chaos tools (Chaos Mesh).
